# Design references

Consulted November 1, 2026:

- https://github.com/gireeshkumarreddy/freefall-portfolio-code — its documented continuous scroll-driven journey inspired the idea-to-work transition. No video frames, source code, or branded imagery were copied into this design.
- https://bruno-simon.com/ — the site's invitation to explore and interact inspired a small optional tactile sculpture, while retaining conventional navigation.
- https://www.henryheffernan.com/ — unavailable through the research tool; not used as a design source.

The folded-paper bloom is original SVG geometry written for this portfolio. Typography uses the already installed local Archivo, Instrument Serif, and Caveat fonts. The project covers are editorial illustrations, explicitly labelled where they are not product screenshots.

The supplied Portfolio Quality Lab PDF is reproduced as a public worksheet at the owner's request, with the original document available for download. It is treated as a design/testing brief and educational content, not as biographical information or proof that checks passed.
