# The Next Page — original story and artwork

The protagonist is an original young Indian boy from a middle-class family in Pune. His journey moves from a family table to learning frontend development, persevering through a broken interface, freelance work, and the next creative opportunity. The supplied professional facts are in `src/profile.ts`. Family scenes, character appearance and dialogue are dramatized, not a claim about Vaibhav’s exact life history or likeness.

The manga portfolio at https://github.com/inesCherif/vol01-manga-portfolio was inspected for chapter-based storytelling and interaction ideas. Its personal artwork, characters, text and identity were not copied. The implementation uses new React components, a new visual identity and original artwork.

## Animation source and license

The follow-up animation integration inspected `src/scripts/scenes/title.ts`, `origin.ts`, `training.ts`, `tabaani.ts`, `finale.ts`, and the `fx` modules in the upstream repository. `src/motion/pageTurn.ts` reuses its MIT-licensed polygon-fold implementation with attribution retained. `livingInk.ts` adapts the living-frame shader ideas to our atlas and adds shared scheduling, visibility control, context-loss fallback and GPU disposal. `mangaMotion.ts` adapts scroll pinning, comic caption wipes, letter stamping, camera cuts, cursor/parallax, reward feedback and page-pile rewind to our new React markup and original story. The upstream MIT notice is preserved in `src/motion/VOL01-LICENSE.txt`.

The ink drips use generated SVG turbulence and CSS shapes. No protected ink textures, illustrations, original character cutouts or personal CV content were copied. Original scene-specific character walking, destructible VOL.01 branding and effects that depend on the reference’s layered illustrations are not represented as exact copies; their techniques are adapted to this site’s own assets.

## Artwork

Generated using the built-in image generation tool. Saved to `public/manga/origin-panels.png`. The source is retained in the tool’s generated-images directory. Six scenes share one consistent character; CSS crops individual scenes from the atlas. The lettering and speech bubbles are real accessible website text rather than words embedded in the illustrations.

### Generation prompt

Use case: illustration-story. Create ONE original manga illustration asset as a precisely aligned 3-column by 2-row sheet of six equal rectangular cinematic panels, landscape overall 1536x1024 or larger. Each panel has no gutters and fills exactly one sixth of the canvas, so website CSS can show a panel individually. Consistent male protagonist: young Indian boy/young man from Pune, short slightly tousled black hair, dark expressive eyes, slim average build, simple white T-shirt and dark open overshirt, canvas backpack. Respectful middle-class family life, modest comfortable apartment, never poverty caricature. Original black Indian coming-of-age manga linework, deep black ink, detailed architectural hatching and screentone, ivory paper, ONLY a tiny burnt orange accent on a notebook in each scene. No words, no speech bubbles, no letters, no numbers, no watermarks, no logos. Six story panels in reading order: TOP LEFT establishing wide view: boy seen from rear standing on apartment rooftop, Pune residential rooftops and distant hills, dawn, wind in overshirt; TOP MIDDLE intimate family scene: boy at compact dining table with supportive middle-aged mother and father, ordinary steel cups and notebooks, warmly supportive dignified home; TOP RIGHT late-night learning: side view same young man at small desk studying code on an older laptop with abstract illegible screen, desk lamp, notebooks, headphones; BOTTOM LEFT setback and resolve: close-up portrait of same young man's determined face looking toward light, pencil held beside sketchbook, bold diagonal speed-line composition; BOTTOM MIDDLE first work: same young man focused working on laptop beside neatly arranged sketches, strong cinematic low angle, code as tiny illegible strokes; BOTTOM RIGHT new horizon: same young man walking forward on a rooftop terrace at sunrise with backpack, city opening out, hopeful composed expression, dynamic manga foreground lines. Professional hand-inked manga, beautiful detailed illustrated environments, consistent recognizable boy across all six panels. No superheroes, no Iron Man, no reference to other artists' characters. Output is website art, not a screenshot or UI.

## Updating the story

Edit `src/mangaStory.ts` to change the narrative. Edit `src/profile.ts` for professional details. Replace the illustration sheet to change character appearance; the artwork is an invented manga protagonist, not based on the supplied Charizard profile image.
